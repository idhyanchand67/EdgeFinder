EdgeFinder

Prop lines vs. recent history
▸ How this works — data sources, hit rate & edge (pp) explained

Hit rate

For each prop, we look at the player's last N games (10 by default) and count how often the actual stat beat the sportsbook's current line. A receiver with an Over/Under 74.5 receiving-yards line who topped 74.5 yards in 8 of his last 10 games has an 80% hit rate.

Edge (pp)

pp = percentage points, the plain arithmetic difference between two percentages. An 80% hit rate against a price implying 62% is "+18pp," not "+18%" - which would mean something different (a relative change, not a difference).

Edge is hit rate minus the win probability the sportsbook's own price implies:

implied probability from American odds: price < 0 -> -price / (-price + 100) price > 0 -> 100 / (price + 100) edge = hit_rate - implied_probability

Why it matters: a prop that's hit 100% of its last 10 games sounds great, but if the book already prices it at -2000 (implying ~95%) because it's a near-certainty - say, a power hitter's Under 0.5 home runs, since going deep in any given game is rare - the real edge is close to zero. The book already "knows." A prop priced near a coinflip (~50% implied) that's actually hit 80% of the time has a genuine +30pp edge - a real gap between the price and recent history. Top 10 and the table's default sort rank by edge, not raw hit rate, for exactly this reason.

Where the data comes from

  • NFL player stats: nflverse - free, public, last 3 seasons
  • NBA / MLB / NHL player stats: ESPN's public boxscore data, backfilled game by game
  • Current prop lines and odds: The Odds API
  • Injury status: ESPN's public injuries feed
  • NFL matchup difficulty: computed from the same nflverse stats - average fantasy points allowed per position, by defense

Matchup (NFL only)

Tough / Average / Favorable, based on how many fantasy points a defense has allowed to that position this window, split into thirds across the league. One proxy signal, not a full model - it doesn't account for pace, game script, or injuries on the defense itself.

Injury tags

Q = questionable / day-to-day - may play a reduced or different role than their recent history reflects. OUT = ruled out, IR, suspended, and similar. OUT players are automatically excluded from Top 10 (still shown, tagged, in the full table below).

Important caveats

  • Not betting advice. This is a research filter, not a signal to bet blind.
  • Edge uses the current posted price, not real historical odds - whether this signal survives against what a book actually charged in the past is still an open question. See the project's backtest for the full methodology and honest results.
  • NFL preseason games are excluded automatically - backup usage doesn't match the regular-season history hit rates are built from.
